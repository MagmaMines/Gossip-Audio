# Privacy

Neural speech generation, encoding and local playback run on-device. This app does not request INTERNET permission or require an account. Text and clips are stored in app-private storage; user-exported files are saved to Downloads and can be shared. Android backup behavior follows the app's backup rules and device settings.

Dictation uses a separate Android recognition service; that service may send audio to its provider even when offline is preferred. Review its privacy policy and installed offline packs. Microphone and notification permissions are optional and requested for the relevant features.
